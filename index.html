        About this guide

        A beginner’s guide to Pohela Boishakh — the first day of the Bengali calendar year (14 April) and a public holiday in Bangladesh. Dates and customs are general cultural knowledge, not an official source; check local announcements for procession times and fair schedules.

        Pohela Boishakh BD — offline personal tool. A cultural guide and celebration planner, not an official source. Bengali year = Gregorian year − 593.
